FIRED-BUT-DIDN'T-HELP (worst failure)
-------------------------------------
1206.1993 \Bar — cs_targeted_fix fired "polyfill injected" but the payload
persisted identical into round 2 (stuck→acceptable_pdf). Mechanism, verified by
hand-reproduced xelatex log:
  - polyfill `\providecommand{\Bar}[1]{\overline{#1}}` lands at main.tex:37,
    BEFORE `\usepackage{amsmath}` (l.195). amsmath:900 does unconditional
    `\def\Bar{\bar}` → clobbers the polyfill.
  - doc loads eulervm (l.199); eulervm.sty:151 `\let\bar\undefined` then only
    re-`\DeclareMathAccent`s `\bar` when \operator@encoding=T1. Under xelatex/TU
    the branch is skipped → \bar stays undefined → `\Bar ->\bar` = dead.
  So: docclass-seam \providecommand cannot survive later package \def/\let.
  For cs owned by real packages the fix needs AtBeginDocument deferral or a
  \renewcommand; here the true missing leaf is \bar (eulervm+TU gap), not \Bar.
